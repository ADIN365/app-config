Parking Notes — Privacy Policy

LazyWorks (the ‘Developer’) respects the privacy of users of Parking Notes (the ‘App’) and provides this Privacy Policy in accordance with the Republic of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Information we process

Parking records and photos are stored primarily on your device. The Developer does not operate a separate server for uploading parking records. However, data may leave your device through the external services and operating-system backups described below.

2. Purposes of collection and use

  • Automatically detecting and saving your parking location
  • Showing your parking location on a map to help you find your car
  • Recording the parking level and photos
  • Displaying parking details in home screen widgets
  • Advertising, ad-removal purchases and rewards, usage and error analysis, and notices and update information

3. Retention, deletion and backups

  • Current parking records remain until you delete them or a new record replaces them for the same vehicle. Photos are cleaned up when replaced or their record is deleted; unreferenced photos may also be removed when the App starts.
  • When parking history is enabled, entries older than your selected period (7, 30, 90 or 365 days; default: 30) are removed when the next history entry is saved, not immediately when the period expires. You can also delete all entries on the history screen.
  • The App allows Android backup and does not specifically exclude its parking database, settings or app-specific photos. Depending on your device, account, backup settings and operating-system limits, these may be included in cloud backups or device transfers and restored after reinstallation or a device change. The Developer cannot directly access or delete these operating-system backups.
  • Uninstalling the App or clearing its data in device settings removes the App’s data on that device. It does not immediately delete existing backups, data already received by external services or Google Play purchase records. Manage backups separately in your device or account settings.
  • External-service data is handled under each service’s retention policies and applicable settings. Crashlytics retains crash traces and related identifiers for 90 days before starting deletion. Retention of analytics, advertising, purchase and access records varies by service and data type and is separate from the App’s parking-history period.

4. External services and data transfers

These services process data when their features run. This includes identifiers and diagnostics collected automatically by SDKs, so we do not claim that all data is anonymous. Depending on the provider and settings, processing may take place outside your country, including in the United States. Provider policies explain retention, transfers and deletion.

  • Google AdMob — Processes IP addresses (which may indicate a general location), advertising and app identifiers, ad views, clicks and other interactions, and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Available identifiers and processing depend on device and advertising settings. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Sends installation identifiers, device, OS and app information, crash stacks, exceptions and diagnostic logs to Google for troubleshooting. If a location lookup fails, the vehicle’s Bluetooth MAC address may be included in an error log. (Firebase)
  • Firebase Analytics — Google processes app-instance and advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location and usage events. The App also sends Bluetooth disconnection signal types (HFP, A2DP or ACL) and time to display the popup. These custom events do not contain parking coordinates, photos, vehicle names or MAC addresses.
  • Google Play — Google Play handles purchases. The App queries purchase status and tokens and acknowledges purchases to apply or restore ad removal. The Developer does not directly receive payment-method details such as card numbers. Ad removal is an advertising benefit; it does not stop all analytics, crash reporting or backups.
  • GitHub — Fetching notices, tips and version information from GitHub or opening this policy sends IP addresses and request information to the hosting provider. Loading remote notice images or opening links also sends access information to the relevant servers. The App does not attach the parking database or photos to notice-configuration requests. (GitHub)
  • Android / Google Play services — With location permission, the App obtains coordinates through Google Play services and requests addresses through Android Geocoder. Coordinates and language may be sent to the device’s geocoding provider. Processing depends on the device’s location services and settings.
  • Maps — When you open a parking location in a map, the App passes its coordinates and a display name to the selected map app, such as Google Maps, NAVER Map, KakaoMap or TMAP. Subsequent processing follows that service’s policy.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete parking records on the main screen, or disable history recording and delete all history on the history screen. Turning off history recording does not itself delete existing entries.
  • Change or revoke location, Bluetooth, camera and other permissions in device settings; this may limit related features. You can also manage advertising IDs and backups in device or account settings. In supported US states, you can use “Ad privacy choices” in the app settings to change whether you allow the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. This choice does not affect your ad-removal purchase or existing free benefits. In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can accept or refuse advertising consent and manage individual choices in the consent form. You can revisit your choices through “Ad privacy choices” in the app settings.
  • The App has no separate switches to disable analytics or crash reporting. Deleting an advertising ID or purchasing ad removal does not stop all external data processing. For external-service data, use the provider’s privacy controls or contact us below. From version 1.3.9, Firebase analytics and crash reporting are enabled only after Google’s regional check confirms that no advertising consent or privacy-choice process is required. In regions where such a process is required, analytics and crash reporting remain disabled even if you consent to advertising. Collection also remains disabled before the regional check or if it fails. Advertising consent is handled separately.
  • Contact the email address below to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing. We review requests under applicable law. The Developer cannot remotely access records stored only on your device; delete those through the App or device settings.
