Find My Car — Privacy Policy

LazyWorks (the ‘Developer’) respects the privacy of users of Find My Car (the ‘App’) and provides this Privacy Policy in accordance with the Republic of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Personal information we collect

The App processes the following information only on your device to provide its services.

InformationPurposeStorage location
Location information (GPS)Automatically saving your parking location and displaying it on a mapLocal database on your device
Bluetooth device information (name and MAC address)Automatically recording parking by detecting connections to and disconnections from your car’s BluetoothLocal database on your device
Camera photosSaving photos of your parking spot when you choose to take themDevice storage

2. Purposes of collection and use

  • Automatically detecting and saving your parking location
  • Showing your parking location on a map to help you find your car
  • Recording the parking level and photos
  • Displaying parking details in home screen widgets

3. Retention and deletion

  • All data is stored only on your device and is not sent to external servers.
  • Parking history is automatically deleted after the retention period you select (7 days to 1 year).
  • Uninstalling the App immediately deletes all data.

4. Third-party services

The App does not provide your personal information to third parties. However, the services below may collect data under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob — Collects advertising identifiers to serve ads (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Collects crash logs to diagnose App errors (without personally identifying information)
  • Firebase Analytics — Collects anonymous App usage statistics, such as Bluetooth signal types and response times, without personally identifying information
  • Google Play — Processes in-app purchases for ad removal

  • You can delete parking records yourself in the App’s settings.
